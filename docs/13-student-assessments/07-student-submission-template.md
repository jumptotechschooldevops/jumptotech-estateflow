<your honest estimate of your own score per category in 06-grading-rubric.md, before the

## Why the authenticity statement matters

This isn't a formality. [06 · Grading rubric](06-grading-rubric.md) lists fabricated
evidence as an automatic fail — the statement exists so there's no ambiguity about what
you're claiming when you submit.

## Checklist before submitting

- [ ] Every task has real command output, not paraphrased
- [ ] Every required screenshot is attached
- [ ] The authenticity statement is filled in, not left as a placeholder
- [ ] Reflection section is honest, including about mistakes
